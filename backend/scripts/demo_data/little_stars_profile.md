# Little Stars Kids Wear — ليتل ستارز لملابس الأطفال

> Fictional company for the demo. All names, numbers and prices are invented.

## Who we are | إحنا مين
Little Stars is a kids-clothing factory in the **10th of Ramadan** industrial city (Sharqia), founded in 2014.
We design and sew everyday and occasion wear for babies and children (0–12 years) and sell **wholesale** to
kids-clothing shops and baby stores in Greater Cairo.

مصنع ليتل ستارز لملابس الأطفال في مدينة العاشر من رمضان. بنصمم ونصنّع لبس أطفال من حديثي الولادة لحد ١٢ سنة،
وبنبيع **جملة** لمحلات ملابس الأطفال ومحلات البيبي في القاهرة والجيزة.

## Offers | العروض
- First order: up to **10%** off (الطلب الأول خصم لحد ١٠٪).
- Orders above 50,000 EGP: free delivery inside Cairo and Giza.
- Payment: cash on delivery or bank transfer; repeat customers can pay 50% on order and 50% on delivery.
- The sales team cannot offer more than 10% without the owner's approval.

## Delivery | التوصيل
- Cairo & Giza: 3–5 working days. Other governorates: 5–7 working days through a shipping company.
- Samples: a sample pack of 6 pieces for 600 EGP, deducted from the first order.

## Why shops buy from us | ليه المحلات بتشتري مننا
- Factory prices (no middleman) and Egyptian cotton.
- Small minimum orders, mixed sizes, fast re-stocking of best sellers.
- Exchange of defective pieces within 14 days.
